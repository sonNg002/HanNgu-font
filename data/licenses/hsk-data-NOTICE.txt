Modifications by HanNgu: grouped into 146 numbered lessons and nine volumes;
merged duplicate word/pronunciation pairs within a lesson; excluded unnumbered
supplementary vocabulary and appendix grammar; selected vocabulary/grammar fields.
Source CSV SHA-256 hashes are recorded in data/hsk-standard.json.
Retrieved 2026-10-16. English definitions are retained in the source snapshot.
Vietnamese display translations were generated with Google Translate on 2026-10-18.
They are machine translations and have not received a complete linguistic review.
The translation mapping is stored in data/vietnamese-translations.json; production
builds apply it without contacting a translation service. The adapted Vietnamese
HSK data retains the CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution and share-alike requirements above.

This is an open companion dataset, not an official publisher-certified digital
edition. Textbook text, illustrations, and audio are not included. Lesson mapping
and linguistic content may contain source errors; report corrections with source.
